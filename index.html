About

I'm an aspiring filmmaker, screenwriter, and actor from Metro Detroit. I create independent short films, write original screenplays, and enjoy bringing stories to life both behind and in front of the camera.

This portfolio is a collection of my work as I continue learning, creating, and growing as an artist.
